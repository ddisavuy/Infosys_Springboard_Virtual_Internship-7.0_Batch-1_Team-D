 📊 **Supply Chain Visibility System with Optimization Analytics**

🚀 **Project Overview**
This project was completed as part of our internship with **Infosys Springboard Virtual Internship 7.0**.

**Supply Chain Visibility System with Optimization Analytics** is an interactive **Data Analytics project** designed to analyze supply chain data and provide meaningful insights into sales, orders, customers, products, delivery operations, geographic markets, costs, discounts, shipping, and profitability. The project uses **Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Microsoft Excel, and Microsoft Teams** to transform the **DataCoSupplyChainDataset** into meaningful insights and an easy-to-understand dashboard experience.

The project demonstrates how **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence** can be used to solve real-world supply chain problems by providing data-driven insights, interactive visualizations, KPI analysis, and meaningful business interpretation.

🚀 **Key Features**

• 📊 **Sales Performance Analysis:**
Analyzes total sales, total orders, total profit, yearly sales trends, customer segments, payment types, and order status.

• 🔍 **Delivery & Shipping Analysis:**
Analyzes on-time delivery, late delivery, average delivery delay, shipping modes, actual shipping days, scheduled shipping days, and delivery status.

• 🧠 **Customer & Product Analysis:**
Provides insights into customer segments, customer contribution, product demand, departments, categories, quantity sold, and average profit per order.

• ⚙️ **Cost & Profitability Analysis:**
Analyzes discount amounts, discount rates, sales, profit ratio, category-level profitability, and regional performance.

• 📈 **Geographic & Order Performance Analysis:**
Analyzes countries, markets, regions, order value, order quantity, shipping-mode distribution, and sales-profit relationships.

📊 **Project Workflow**

1. **Data Collection** – The DataCoSupplyChainDataset was used as the primary source of supply chain information, containing approximately 80,519 records related to orders, customers, products, sales, shipping, delivery, geography, discounts, and profit.

2. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing** – The dataset was examined and prepared using Power Query. Required fields were identified, data types were checked, and relevant data was cleaned and transformed for further analysis.

3. **Data Analysis** – The prepared dataset was analyzed using Power BI and DAX to calculate important metrics related to sales, orders, customers, products, delivery, discounts, costs, and profitability.

4. **Visualization / Development** – Interactive Power BI dashboards were developed progressively through four milestones, resulting in **seven dashboard pages** containing KPI cards, charts, tables, maps, slicers, and analytical visuals.

5. **Insights & Interpretation** – The final dashboard was used to identify trends and patterns in sales, customer contribution, product performance, delivery efficiency, geographic markets, shipping modes, discounts, costs, and profitability.
